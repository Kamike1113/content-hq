## Analysis
1. **Shorts get views, long-form gets subscribers.** Top 4 by views are all shorts, but ~90% of subs gained come from long-form guides/audits.
2. **"Trend + curiosity" hooks outperform.** "Is this the next ecom trend in 2026?" (711) and "New ecom trend in 2026?" (366) beat generic titles like "A/B tests that ripped for our clients" (165–202) by 2–4x.
3. **Personality content travels furthest.** The #1 video is an 11-second agency-life clip with 89% retention — personal beats purely tactical for reach.
4. **Long-form retention is the weak point.** Avg view % on long videos is 13–18% (viewers leave in the first 1–2 min). Better cold-opens/hooks would compound the subscriber engine.
5. **Older SEO-style uploads (2025 audits) get near-zero ongoing views** — the channel currently grows on push (shorts velocity), not search.

## Recommendations
1. Weekly cadence: 3–4 shorts with trend/curiosity/personality hooks + 1 long-form guide or audit (the sub engine).
2. Rewrite long-form intros: state payoff in first 15 seconds; test cold-open with the result ("This checkout change added $200K/mo").
3. Repeat the "agency life" personality format — highest reach + retention on the channel.
4. Title formula that's working: question + year/trend ("Is this the next ecom trend in 2026?"). Apply to upcoming shorts.
