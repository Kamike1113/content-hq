## Cross-competitor insights
1. **"Template revealed / step-by-step breakdown" is the killer format in Andrei's niche.** Carl's only two 100K+ videos are both exactly this. Specific artifact + reveal + walkthrough.
2. **Money-credibility in titles works:** "$100M Brand", "$5M by 25", "$15M Generated", "$25M profitable" — real numbers as proof, right in the title.
3. **Personal income/story videos are outliers even on tactical channels** (Carl's "How I've made $5M by 25" = 2x median, most-commented). Matches Andrei's own personality-content pattern.
4. **Evergreen full tutorials/free courses compound for years** (Max's Klaviyo tutorials keep pulling 50–100K). A "Full CRO / A/B testing course" would be Andrei's version.
5. **"I studied X so you don't have to" study format** is trending in 2026 (Max's $1B DTC video, PBL's Top-100 creators video).
6. **Shorts don't build these channels** — every one of these creators wins on long-form. Confirms shorts = discovery, long-form = subs.
7. **Cadence matters less than packaging:** PBL gets 8x more subs-per-video than Max with 5% of the output.
